We wanted to keep the selection process as transparent and consistent as possible. For this round, we decided to use GitHub as the primary and only evaluation source. While we initially considered other platforms such as LinkedIn, we found that LinkedIn activity varied significantly across participants and did not provide a consistent basis for evaluating student developers. Some participants may have limited or no activity on LinkedIn despite being active builders. GitHub, on the other hand, is where developers typically showcase their code, projects, contributions, and technical work. Therefore, to maintain a consistent evaluation methodology across all participants, we based the shortlist entirely on publicly available GitHub information.
